# Weather audio sources

All source recordings below are released under CC0 / public domain and were
edited, filtered, mixed, normalized, and re-encoded for Zuujis.

- Rain loops by Ylmir: https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
- Wind recordings by IgnasD: https://opengameart.org/content/wind
- Wind whoosh loop by SketchMan3: https://opengameart.org/content/wind-whoosh-loop
- Thunder and stone impacts from "100 CC0 SFX #2" by rubberduck:
  https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2

Derived files:

- `rain.ogg`, `storm.ogg` — processed rain recordings.
- `snow.ogg`, `blizzard.ogg`, `dust-storm.ogg` — processed wind layers.
- `hurricane.ogg` — mixed wind and heavy rain.
- `hail.ogg` — mixed rain and stone/ice-like impacts.
- `thunder.ogg` — processed thunder transient.

Fog and meteor shower intentionally have no audio track.


- Thunder Sound Effects pack promoted by Happy Soul Music Library (40-track
  collection moderated from CC0 Freesound sources):
  https://happysoulmusic.com/electricity-and-thunder-sound-effects/

Additional derived files:

- `thunder/distant-01.ogg` through `thunder/distant-14.ogg`
- `thunder/medium-01.ogg` through `thunder/medium-14.ogg`
- `thunder/close-01.ogg` through `thunder/close-12.ogg`

These 40 recordings were trimmed, loudness-balanced by distance band, converted
to stereo 48 kHz Vorbis, and curated from the 40-track CC0 OGG collection.
